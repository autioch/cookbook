## Against the family's own sites

Polish recipes measure heaped and packed; USDA measures level. The gap is large:

| Site and recipe                                                                     | Says                                | Per measure | USDA       |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ----------- | ---------- |
| [AniaGotuje, ciastka owsiane](https://aniagotuje.pl/przepis/proste-ciastka-owsiane) | 1,5 szklanki płatków = 200 g        | 133 g       | 86 g       |
| same                                                                                | 2 czubate łyżki mąki = do 45 g      | ≤ 22 g      | ~8 g level |
| [Moja Delicja, ciasto bananowe](https://www.mojadelicja.pl/ciasto-bananowe/)        | 1,5 szklanki mąki tortowej = 240 g  | 160 g       | 132 g      |
| same                                                                                | 0,5–0,75 szklanki cukru = 100–150 g | 200 g       | 211 g      |

**So:** a single table will be wrong for someone. Proposal: the recipe states grams; the glass
beside it says „ok.”; the family's own glass, weighed once per product, replaces USDA's where it
differs (her kitchen is the measure that matters).

## Tins and pots (D10)

Plain arithmetic, no source needed: a rectangular tin's area is a × b, a round tin's π × r².
Amounts scale by the ratio of areas when the depth stays the same: a 24 cm round tin (452 cm²) to
a 26 cm one (531 cm²) is × 1,17. A pot by volume: π × r² × h. Moje Wypieki does the tin sum per
recipe; Niebo na talerzu has a widget ([comparison.md](comparison.md) A9).
