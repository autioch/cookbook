# Comparison — what other recipe sites have

Research input, November 2026, for the owners' review: not rules and not agreed work. What other
sites put on the **whole site** (part A) and on **one recipe's page** (part B), set against what
Nasza kuchnia has, and how the ideas in [ideas.md](ideas.md) look on the same sites (part C). Rows
are numbered (A1, B5…) so a review can point at them. The sites are the ones the family cooks from
(the owner's links); each was read from its home page and the recipe page the owner linked, by its
text and its page source. Anything else on a site is unread.

Key: ✓ has it · ~ partly · – no · ? couldn't tell. **Take?** is the agent's proposal, for the
owners to pick: **yes**, **maybe**, **no**, **later** (agreed for after the proof of concept), or
**have** (the site does it already). The owner's wife picks on a temporary page on a deploy
preview; her answers come back into [backlog.md](../backlog.md).

## The gap

**Nobody starts from what's at home.** Of the 18 sites, several let you browse by one ingredient
(A2: 5 fully, 8 partly), none takes several products at once and ranks recipes by what's missing.
SuperCook does, in English, across the web. That is the site's finder, already built.

**Nobody scales to your servings.** Every page prints one fixed batch. Moje Wypieki converts a cake
to another tin; that is the nearest (B5).

What the usual recipe page also skips:

- **A clean page**: 14 of 18 carry ads and 4 more partly; almost all show a cookie banner and a
  newsletter box (A13, A16, A17). Four open with a long story before the recipe (B1).
- **Keeping the screen on**: two sites (B13), both with one button.
- **Storage**: how long the jam or the cheese keeps, on two (B16).

What it adds that this site rules out: health and diet claims on 6 sites, partly on 5 more (A15:
„dba o linię”, „korzystne dla serca”); shops, affiliate links and brands (A14), Lidl and Winiary
being shops themselves; calories (A20).

## What stands out

- **The big sites are loose.** KS, AG and Lidl have search, ratings and accounts; ads and a cookie
  banner sit around every recipe. A page with only the recipe already reads differently.
- **The good blogs have the small things right**: AG gives cups and grams, storage and
  substitutions; MD and MW keep the screen on; MD ticks off ingredients.
- **Story first is common on the long-form sites** (JA, JP, KO, PY): the recipe is a scroll away.
- **Health talk is normal**, from „zdrowa przekąska dla dzieci” (AG) to diet phases (JP).
- **Two gaps only this site fills**: the finder (A4) and, once built, scaling (B5) and the family's
  log (C6).
