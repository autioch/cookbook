# Ideas — not agreed (2026-11)

Input for the owners, not rules. Each moves to [backlog.md](../backlog.md) once agreed.

**From the owner's first brief:** a knowledge base; how hot it is; whether the children liked it;
the time it takes; a shopping list; a look at how other sites do it (the owners will send links).

**The agent's proposals:**

- **Scale a recipe to servings** — 2 people instead of 4; tinctures has the arithmetic
  (`src/recipes/Scale/` there): units would need fixed names (g, ml, szt., łyżka…).
- **Shopping list from several recipes**, summed, the choice in the URL (as tinctures' Lista
  zakupów); printed or sent to a phone.
- **A week's plan** — pick dinners for the week, get one shopping list.
- **„Co zrobić z resztek”** — the finder's ranking, but for leftovers (cooked rice, stale bread).
- **Seasonal** — what's in season this month (strawberries in June, plums in September), each
  sourced.
- **Family log** — when it was cooked, for whom (guests, a birthday), what changed; like
  tinctures' Dziennik.
- **Tags beyond meals** — wegetariańskie, na szybko (≤ 30 min), dla gości, z piekarnika, na
  zapas (freezes).
- **Photos** of the family's own dishes, small and light.
- **Substitutions** — "no buttermilk? milk and lemon juice", in the knowledge base.
- **Kitchen measures** — a glass, a spoon in grams, per product, sourced.
- **A typed product box** in the finder once the checkbox list grows past a screen.
- **Search by name** over the list.
- **Print a cookbook** — chosen recipes as one PDF, a notebook for a gift.
